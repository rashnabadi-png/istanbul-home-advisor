Common questions

Can foreigners buy property in Istanbul?

Yes. Citizens of most countries can buy property in Turkey, with some limits on certain areas and nationalities. We check this for you before you reserve.

Does buying a home give me Turkish citizenship?

Some purchases can qualify for citizenship by investment, but the rules and checks have become stricter. Eligibility is confirmed case by case by a licensed lawyer before you buy.

Do I need to come to Istanbul to buy?

Not always. Many steps can be done remotely with a power of attorney, but we recommend visiting before you buy. We can arrange video tours first.

What does the advisor cost?

The advisor is free to use. Any service fees are explained to you in writing before you commit to anything.

What happens to my personal data?

We only save your details if you agree, under Turkey's personal data protection law (KVKK). You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Ask the advisor anything, any time. When you are ready, an agent will take it from there.
